# Plan a joint campaign

Find partner fit and structure a shared marketing idea.

## Inputs

Your audience, offer, partner options, and objective.

## Steps

1. Assess where the audiences overlap and what each partner contributes.
2. Define a shared idea, audience benefit, commitments, owners, and outcome measures.
3. Return a campaign brief and an outreach draft when requested.

## Deliverable

A partner shortlist or a joint campaign plan.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
