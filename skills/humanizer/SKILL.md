# Make copy sound more natural

Revise stiff or generic phrasing while preserving the message.

## Inputs

Draft copy and an example of your voice, if available.

## Steps

1. Preserve the source meaning, facts, and any required terminology.
2. Replace stiff transitions and repeated abstractions with specific verbs and the user's natural rhythm.
3. Return the edited copy and flag source ambiguities that affect meaning.

## Deliverable

A cleaner version in the intended voice.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
