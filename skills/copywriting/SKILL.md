# Rewrite marketing copy

Improve the message on a homepage, landing page, or campaign.

## Inputs

Existing copy, buyer, offer, and the next action.

## Steps

1. Identify the decision the copy must earn and where the reader entered.
2. Draft a specific promise, credible proof, short objection handling, and a concrete action. Preserve the user's brand voice.
3. Return a ready-to-use version and a short list of claims to validate.

## Deliverable

Revised copy with key changes explained.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
