# Review your own ad account

Identify the strongest and weakest patterns in your current creative.

## Inputs

Ad export, creative samples, and reporting period.

## Steps

1. Match supplied files and ad IDs to one export and attribution window.
2. Compare spend, reach, conversions, and performance within comparable periods. Separate strong creative signals from low-volume results.
3. Identify controls, format patterns, and opportunities for a one-variable test.

## Decision checks

Choose the decision and analysis grain first. Match each ad ID to its asset, audience, dates, currency and attribution window. Recompute CPA from total spend and purchases across disjoint rows. Averaging row CPAs can reverse the ranking.

Compare like cohorts after allowing for conversion lag. Apply the account's documented winner definition, then separate a profitable result from the explanation for it. Where the winning asset is missing, report the financial observation and the evidence needed to explain the creative.

If an authorized course pack is available, consult Winner definitions and the Growth Guide log for this decision.

Compare the eligible population, attribution window and net contribution after mature refunds. Establish whether a comparison can support an incremental claim. See [Offers, positioning and acquisition](../evolve-skill/references/book-offer-decisions.md).

## Deliverable

A creative baseline for the next test.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.

## Decision checks for this skill

Join evidence to the actual delivered file and offer, with period and audience attached. Mark mismatched versions as incomparable.
