# Design a marketing test

Define a clean comparison for a campaign or page change.

## Inputs

Hypothesis, current version, candidate variant, and metrics.

## Steps

1. State the decision, metric, comparison unit, and meaningful outcome threshold.
2. Define control, variant, audience rules, tracking, traffic expectations, and stop conditions.
3. List practical validity checks and what to do if the result is inconclusive.

## Deliverable

A test setup with success and guardrail metrics.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
