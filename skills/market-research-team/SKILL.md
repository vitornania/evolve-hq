# Run a research sprint

Review customer and competitor evidence before proposing ad angles.

## Inputs

Product, target market, and the decision to make.

## Steps

1. Assign distinct jobs: one researcher collects first-person customer evidence, another compares competitor offers and claims, and a reviewer checks the original sources. Give each the decision, audience, product facts and required source IDs. With one operator, run separate passes and identify the review as self-review.
2. Merge findings by buyer situation or market question. Preserve actual disagreements: a competitor promise is evidence of market messaging, a customer question signals uncertainty, and an observed product test supports only its conditions. Reopen the most consequential source before selecting an angle.
3. Hand off one defensible decision and its strongest alternative. If the task calls for a creative portfolio, compare distinct buyer or belief hypotheses and identify what each would teach. Name what evidence would separate them, who could obtain it and which downstream claims are usable now. A missing source blocks the claim that depends on it; other supported work can proceed.

## Deliverable

Return a compact decision record with the evidence owners, verified facts, competing readings and next test. If running inside the installed team library, carry the source register into its research record.
