# Run a research sprint

Review customer and competitor evidence before proposing ad angles.

## Inputs

Product, target market, and the decision to make.

## Steps

1. Collect customer voice and competitor offers as independent workstreams with stable evidence IDs.
2. Compare offers in the same market and audit every material finding for wording and context. Label a single-agent review as self-review.
3. Turn reviewed evidence into up to three distinct angles with hypotheses and explain the next decision.

## Deliverable

A source-linked brief and up to three ad angles.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
