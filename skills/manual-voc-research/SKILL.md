# Analyze supplied customer quotes

Find patterns in reviews, comments, and customer quotes you provide.

## Inputs

Pasted reviews, customer quotes, or discussion excerpts.

## Steps

1. Assign a short source ID to each supplied passage and preserve its wording.
2. Code the quotes by problem, trigger, workaround, objection, and desired outcome. Preserve rare but meaningful counterexamples.
3. Recommend opportunities supported by examples. State what can be inferred from the sample.

## Deliverable

Themes and opportunities linked to the source quotes.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
