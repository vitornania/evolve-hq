# Analyze supplied customer quotes

Find patterns in reviews, comments, and customer quotes you provide.

## Inputs

Pasted reviews, customer quotes, or discussion excerpts.

## Steps

1. Assign IDs to whole source units with speaker, context, date and origin when known. Preserve verbatim excerpts separately from interpretations. A quote repeated by another account remains one underlying account of the experience. Mark unclear authorship.
2. Code the raw corpus before comparing it to existing summaries. Link the triggering situation, workaround, desired change, buyer belief and objection to actual excerpts. Keep explicit disagreement and customers who do not have the problem. Report independent support and coverage if making frequency claims.
3. For intake-only work, leave creative drafts empty. For synthesis, propose an angle territory as a hypothesis with supporting and contrary IDs. Separate what buyers want from what this product has demonstrated. If the corpus is empty, return the collection gap; if it is small, report what it can support without presenting a market-wide pattern.

## Deliverable

Return a source-linked quote bank, implications and open questions. On an exact workbook or local Evolve synthesis pass, consult the installed evidence and analysis contract and manual VOC synthesis prompt; keep raw and interpreted fields separate.
