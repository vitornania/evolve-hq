# Plan post-purchase offers

Design a useful upsell or experience after checkout.

## Inputs

Products, margins, typical order, and current funnel.

## Steps

1. Start with the existing order, complementary benefit, margin, and relevance.
2. Draft one follow-up offer with clear total price and terms. Consider fulfillment and refunds.
3. Specify the position, copy, consent where relevant, and guardrails for the test.

## Deliverable

A post-purchase plan, copy, and tests.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
