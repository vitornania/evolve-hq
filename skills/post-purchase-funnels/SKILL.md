# Plan post-purchase offers

Design a useful upsell or experience after checkout.

## Inputs

Products, margins, typical order, and current funnel.

## Steps

1. Map what the buyer already paid for, the complementary job, exposure point and accept/decline paths. Check SKU compatibility, eligibility and fulfillment. Keep the original order accessible, and specify the additional charge and opt-in before acceptance.
2. When deciding what to change, start with product or quantity and sequence, then price and terms, then message and interface. Use the earliest uncertain decision that will materially change contribution. Write one clear offer with relevant proof, total price, timing and explicit skip. Never imply that the first purchase was incomplete or secretly include an extra charge. For a declined offer, propose another step only if it solves a separate unresolved need.
3. Separate eligible completed orders, actual offer views, accepts and fulfilled units. Report take rate with its denominator. Evaluate net additional contribution per eligible order after variable costs, reversals and service load; for incrementality compare to an eligible holdout over the same window. Treat downstream repeat purchase or LTV as unknown until measured.

Distinguish friction at a specific touchpoint from loss of ongoing value. Check an actual use or cancellation sequence before choosing a retention intervention. See [Buyer behavior and evidence](../evolve-skill/references/book-customer-decisions.md).

## Deliverable

Return the observable funnel, offer copy, unit economics and next experiment. Use the installed source index for the relevant funnel or platform route when available.
