# Reduce customer cancellations

Find likely reasons for cancellation and plan retention fixes.

## Inputs

Cancellation reasons, product terms, and customer segments.

## Steps

1. Group supplied cancellation reasons by lifecycle stage and customer segment.
2. Connect each candidate fix to evidence and practical effort. Distinguish preventable churn from an informed cancellation decision.
3. Recommend small retention experiments and measure downstream satisfaction or refunds.

## Deliverable

Retention ideas prioritized by evidence.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
