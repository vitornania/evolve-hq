# Reduce customer cancellations

Find likely reasons for cancellation and plan retention fixes.

## Inputs

Cancellation reasons, product terms, and customer segments.

## Steps

1. Group supplied cancellation reasons by lifecycle stage and customer segment.
2. Connect each candidate fix to evidence and practical effort. Distinguish preventable churn from an informed cancellation decision.
3. Recommend small retention experiments and measure downstream satisfaction or refunds.

## Match each intervention to the cause of loss

1. Separate voluntary cancel intent, completed cancellations, involuntary payment failures and eventual recoveries. Use subscribers due to renew or eligible cancellation requests as the denominator that fits the question. A decline can be retried and a request can be withdrawn.
2. Map each observed reason to one relevant remedy the operator can honor. Excess supply may call for a skip or a later shipment. A failed payment calls for a clear payment-update path and appropriate retry policy. Keep cancellation accessible and document the actual extended renewal date or billing terms.
3. Measure retained paid renewals over a stated window, alongside retry recovery, complaints and refunds. An accepted save offer is an intermediate event. Report expected lifetime value separately from realized contribution.

## Deliverable

Retention ideas prioritized by evidence.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
