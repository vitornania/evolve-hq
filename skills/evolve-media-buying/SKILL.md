# Review paid campaign decisions

Evaluate the evidence for keeping, changing, or scaling campaigns.

## Inputs

Campaign export, attribution window, and economics.

## Steps

1. Align the time window and attribution definitions across all supplied figures.
2. Compare spend, attributed revenue, acquisition cost, and economics. Distinguish low sample and blended-rate effects.
3. Recommend whether to hold, test, or change spend with an explicit decision rule.

## Deliverable

A recommendation with risks and next checks.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
