# Improve your app listing

Review how an App Store or Google Play listing converts.

## Inputs

App listing URL or screenshots, category, and target buyer.

## Steps

1. Review the supplied listing in its market and note observable metadata and screenshots.
2. Improve the first screenshots, copy, proof, and keyword relevance for the target buyer without inventing claims.
3. Prioritize revisions and propose a clean listing experiment.

## Deliverable

Listing copy, image direction, and testing ideas.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
