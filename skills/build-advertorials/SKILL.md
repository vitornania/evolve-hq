# Draft an advertorial

Write a long-form page that follows through on an ad’s promise.

## Inputs

Ad angle, offer, buyer, and substantiated proof.

## Steps

1. Connect the ad's promise to the landing page opening.
2. Write a structured story or explanation with verified proof, key objections, clear terms, and a next action.
3. Identify needed assets and check mobile hierarchy, disclosure, and message match.

## Deliverable

A structured advertorial draft and proof gaps.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
