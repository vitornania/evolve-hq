# Write Evolve ad copy

Turn an approved brief into copy for a specific ad format.

## Inputs

Approved creative brief, product facts, and voice.

## Steps

1. Start from the approved brief and identify its proof and target action.
2. Offer hook alternatives that keep the same promise. Move from the buyer's tension to evidence, offer, and action.
3. Review claim support, tone, format limits, and mobile readability. Supply ready-to-paste copy.

## Deliverable

Ad copy with hooks and a call to action.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
