# Write Evolve ad copy

Turn an approved brief into copy for a specific ad format.

## Inputs

Approved creative brief, product facts, and voice.

## Steps

1. Start from the approved brief and identify its proof and target action.
2. Offer hook alternatives that keep the same promise. Move from the buyer's tension to evidence, offer, and action.
3. Review claim support, tone, format limits, and mobile readability. Supply ready-to-paste copy.

## Decision checks

Choose an opening the reader's current awareness can support. Name the buyer's situated problem, what would have to change to solve it, and the verified product action that could enable that change. Show an observable detail before asking the reader to accept a stronger conclusion.

Make the promise proportional to the evidence. A category comment can inform the problem description. A product endorsement requires genuine product experience, accurate attribution and the right to use it. Translate visual evidence into buyer-facing action. Describe what the person can do or see with the product. Keep phrases about what the supplied photo or product sheet shows in the source notes whenever the advertising concept does not call for that narration. Close with one action, verified terms and a destination where the reader can take it.

If an authorized course pack is available, consult Evolve Master Prompt, static brief and Video Script prompt for this decision.

## Deliverable

Ad copy with hooks and a call to action.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
