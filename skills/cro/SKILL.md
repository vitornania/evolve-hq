# Improve page conversions

Diagnose buying friction and prioritize changes for a page or funnel.

## Inputs

A page URL, screenshots, analytics, or funnel context.

## Steps

1. Follow the intended shopper journey from arrival to the next action.
2. Ground each friction finding in a page section, screenshot, or measured step. Separate visual observations and test hypotheses.
3. Return prioritized changes, implementation notes, one primary metric, and a guardrail.

## Separate a broken experience from a conversion hypothesis

1. Walk the journey from actual entry promise through the rendered page, offer and checkout in the affected device, channel and variant. Record reproducible observations and reconcile price, shipping, availability and eligibility against the source-of-truth offer. A broken or misleading price merits correction before experimentation.
2. Calculate the largest loss from observable events with their eligible denominators and same period. Treat friction inferred from screenshots as a hypothesis until supported by behavior, feedback or a controlled test. Isolate device, source and offer changes that could explain the difference.
3. Make one prioritized recommendation with the affected surface, owner, expected buyer behavior and evidence. For an experiment specify the control, single change and primary outcome plus a quality or margin guardrail. For a repair, verify the corrected journey and tracking directly.

## Deliverable

Prioritized conversion ideas and hypotheses to test.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
