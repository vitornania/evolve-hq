# Prepare creative for production

Convert a brief into a practical editing or design handoff.

## Inputs

Approved brief, format, available assets, and constraints.

## Steps

1. Verify the approved concept, script, target format, and source media.
2. Write a shot-by-shot or layer-by-layer plan with timing, crops, captions, audio, and version naming.
3. Return the handoff, dependencies, and quality checklist for the editor or designer.

## Deliverable

A production plan, shot list, or editing handoff.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
