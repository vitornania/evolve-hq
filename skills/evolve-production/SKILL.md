# Prepare creative for production

Convert a brief into a practical editing or design handoff.

## Inputs

Approved brief, format, available assets, and constraints.

## Steps

1. Verify the approved concept, script, target format, and source media.
2. Write a shot-by-shot or layer-by-layer plan with timing, crops, captions, audio, and version naming.
3. Return the handoff, dependencies, and quality checklist for the editor or designer.

## Decision checks

Confirm the identity and usage status of the product, imagery, voice and sound. For video, assign a time range, visual evidence, spoken line and caption to each important beat. For a static, specify copy hierarchy and required visual proof at the final placement size.

Inspect the rendered output at phone size with sound off where relevant. Verify product identity, legibility, offer, CTA, destination and the claim implied by the visuals. Report which artifact was actually rendered and what remains a production specification.

If an authorized course pack is available, consult Static templates, image briefs and the Video Script prompt for this decision.

## Deliverable

A production plan, shot list, or editing handoff.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
