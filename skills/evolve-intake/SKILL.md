# Set up a product brief

Clarify what you sell, who buys it, and what you need to decide.

## Inputs

A product link, draft brief, or description.

## Steps

1. Separate known product facts from assumptions and open claims.
2. Record buyer, market, offer, price, margin assumptions, buying goal, and available proof.
3. Prioritize three unanswered questions that could change the decision.

## Deliverable

A product brief and research questions.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
