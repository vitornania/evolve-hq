# Review ad performance

Analyze one ad or run a batch-level creative review.

## Inputs

Ad files, results, and the decision you need to make.

## Steps

1. Review the ad opening, message, demonstration, proof, and CTA.
2. Join observations to supplied measures using stable creative IDs. Separate correlation from causal claims.
3. Return patterns worth repeating, a shortlist of revisions, and one next creative test.

## Decision checks

Put spend, purchases and revenue beside the creative ID, comparison dates and attribution window. Where available, compare rates at the same funnel step, from impression to outbound click, click to landing visit, and visit to purchase. Use the matching denominator at each step.

For video metrics with compatible play definitions, report three-second plays per impression, ThruPlays per impression, and ThruPlays per three-second play. The last rate conditions on viewers who reached the hook threshold. Label the lengths and completion rule before comparing cuts, and treat a zero denominator as undefined. Use the largest divergence to locate a question about the opening or bridge; check purchase evidence separately.

Explain which observation supports the diagnosis and which other change could explain it, such as a placement shift, offer change or slow page. Recommend the smallest follow-up comparison that separates the two explanations.

For a requested Evolve outcome, use the owner’s applicable first-week spend-share and growth rules. Under campaign-optimized allocation, qualifying share plus qualifying growth is a Breakthrough; qualifying share with measured growth below threshold is a Spend Winner. Below the share threshold, use the agreed KPI to distinguish KPI Winner from Loser; scale data cannot change that branch. If high share is known but growth is unknown, report the qualifying share and leave Spend Winner versus Breakthrough unresolved. Compare actual spending over matched windows; an unchanged budget setting alone does not establish flat spend. Missing asset files limit creative interpretation separately from a measured outcome. State unresolved conditions consistently, including in the opening conclusion.

Apply the bundled method to routine reviews. Consult the original Ad Learnings prompt and winner definitions when an authorized pack is available. Verification against a specifically requested original requires that source.

## Reconcile the plan with the delivered ad

For a creative postmortem, pair the original brief with the actual opening and body. Identify the awareness the opening requires, emotional tone and intensity, buyer situation and desire. Trace the argument for buying, the product action that would produce the benefit, the evidence for believing it, and any real consequence of delay. Inspect the in-ad opening separately from the platform caption. Record the material mismatch or weak link with its line or frame. A performance explanation remains a hypothesis until a comparison can distinguish it from competing explanations. When running a collaborative debrief, retrieve the original brief, invite the operator to correct your interpretation and attribute their explanation. If they are unavailable, retain the hypothesis as unconfirmed and identify the observation that would distinguish it. A narrow metric diagnosis only needs the relevant part of this analysis.

## Deliverable

Findings and the next creative tests.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
