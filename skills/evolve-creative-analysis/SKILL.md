# Review ad performance

Analyze one ad or run a batch-level creative review.

## Inputs

Ad files, results, and the decision you need to make.

## Steps

1. Review the ad opening, message, demonstration, proof, and CTA.
2. Join observations to supplied measures using stable creative IDs. Separate correlation from causal claims.
3. Return patterns worth repeating, a shortlist of revisions, and one next creative test.

## Decision checks

Put spend, purchases and revenue beside the creative ID, comparison dates and attribution window. Where available, compare rates at the same funnel step, from impression to outbound click, click to landing visit, and visit to purchase. Use the matching denominator at each step.

Explain which observation supports the diagnosis and which other change could explain it, such as a placement shift, offer change or slow page. Recommend the smallest follow-up comparison that separates the two explanations.

If an authorized course pack is available, consult Ad Learnings analysis prompt and winner definitions for this decision.

## Deliverable

Findings and the next creative tests.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
