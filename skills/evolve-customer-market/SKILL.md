# Find customer buying patterns

Collect triggers, objections, alternatives, and customer language.

## Inputs

Product, target market, and any reviews or interviews.

## Steps

1. Record buying triggers, objections, failed alternatives, and desired outcomes as distinct themes.
2. Use short quotes only when their actual wording and context are available. Record date, region, and source.
3. Compare patterns across independent sources and note contradictions and sample limits.

## Decision checks

Capture customer words with a locator, date, product context and buyer status when known. Keep exact quotation, paraphrase and inferred motivation distinguishable. Count independent people or sources before calling a theme recurring.

Record counterexamples and the circumstances that might explain them. Turn a repeated situation into a testable buyer hypothesis and name the evidence that would confirm or weaken it. Keep category complaints out of product-specific proof.

If an authorized course pack is available, consult Company Research Document, Desire Hunt and Desire Calendar for this decision.

When several motives fit a quote, reconstruct the last real choice and its alternatives. Identify which buyer answer or observed action would separate the explanations. See [Buyer behavior and evidence](../evolve-skill/references/book-customer-decisions.md).

Apply the relevant checks in [Choose the next creative or research test](../evolve-skill/references/market-and-creative-test-decisions.md#choose-the-next-creative-or-research-test).

## Deliverable

Dated findings with supporting sources.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
