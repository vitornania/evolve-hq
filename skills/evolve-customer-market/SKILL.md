# Find customer buying patterns

Collect triggers, objections, alternatives, and customer language.

## Inputs

Product, target market, and any reviews or interviews.

## Steps

1. Record buying triggers, objections, failed alternatives, and desired outcomes as distinct themes.
2. Use short quotes only when their actual wording and context are available. Record date, region, and source.
3. Compare patterns across independent sources and note contradictions and sample limits.

## Deliverable

Dated findings with supporting sources.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
