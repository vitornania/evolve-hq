# Write a creative brief

Build an Evolve brief for an original ad, a reference, or a variation.

## Inputs

Approved angle and any source ad, offer, and constraints.

## Steps

1. Choose a brief for a new concept, reference adaptation, or a variation of a control.
2. Capture audience, buyer tension, single message, proof, shots or layout, and CTA. Record asset constraints and any claims still awaiting approval.
3. Add the test hypothesis and handoff checklist with stable source IDs.

## Decision checks

Identify the learning question before selecting the template. Mark the lines, visual roles, offer terms and control elements that must stay exact. Separate proposed replacements from approved copy so a producer can act without guessing.

For the course video-script route, check the saved product overview, mechanism research, new-information findings, and customer data. Route missing findings to Insights or Customer Market and mark any resulting concept provisional.

Give a concrete first frame or layout, the proof asset with its source ID, and the click destination. For each requested slot or shot, specify what the viewer should understand and how the asset supports that takeaway. Flag which missing asset would prevent production.

If buyer research shows people want the outcome but question the product, give the producer the specific disbelief, the source behind it and the shot or evidence that answers it. Keep any material effort or condition visible in the handoff. For a buyer who has already seen and accepted the demonstration, use the available space for their remaining buying question.

If an authorized course pack is available, consult Ideation, Imitation and Iteration brief templates for this decision.

When comparing leads, identify the expectation each creates and the evidence the body must supply. Test a wording change against the same buying reason. See [Copy and proof decisions](../evolve-skill/references/book-copy-decisions.md).

Apply the relevant checks in [Choose a format from buyer and product evidence](../evolve-skill/references/market-and-creative-test-decisions.md#choose-the-format-from-buyer-and-product-evidence).

## Deliverable

A brief in the appropriate Evolve format.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.

## Decision checks for this skill

Lock the exact hook, SKU, offer and deliverable. Name visible proof, source asset and what must stay fixed in the test.
