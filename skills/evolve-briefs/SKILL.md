# Write a creative brief

Build an Evolve brief for an original ad, a reference, or a variation.

## Inputs

Approved angle and any source ad, offer, and constraints.

## Steps

1. Choose a brief for a new concept, reference adaptation, or a variation of a control.
2. Capture audience, buyer tension, single message, proof, shots or layout, and CTA. Record asset constraints and any claims still awaiting approval.
3. Add the test hypothesis and handoff checklist with stable source IDs.

## Deliverable

A brief in the appropriate Evolve format.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
