# Grow a community

Plan how to attract and support a community around the product.

## Inputs

Audience, existing community, goals, and available time.

## Steps

1. Define the member problem, participation loop, and core activity.
2. Design a simple cadence, norms, moderation ownership, and ways to hear participant needs.
3. Return a launch plan and metrics that reflect genuine activity and member value.

## Measure the member’s reason to come back

1. Define the member-to-member exchange worth repeating. Map the entry trigger, first useful contribution, response the member receives and reason for returning. Choose a format and owner capable of providing that response consistently.
2. Track eligible joiners, first contributors, contributors receiving a meaningful reply, and returning contributors within timed cohorts. Use first contributors as the denominator for contribution retention. Report member value separately from referred customers or sales.
3. Choose one intervention at the largest observed break, such as getting an unanswered first post a relevant reply. Set an observable follow-up window. Obtain permission before reusing member language as a public testimonial.

Use recurring member questions and workarounds to choose the next contribution. Keep members who engaged, joined, and bought as distinct cohorts. [Buyer evidence](../evolve-skill/references/book-customer-decisions.md)

## Deliverable

A community plan and practical operating cadence.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
