# Plan partnership ads

Prepare a test using creator or partner ads.

## Inputs

Creator or partner context, assets, and campaign goal.

## Steps

1. Confirm the planned partner fit, creative contribution, and terms to verify.
2. Draft the message, proof, deliverables, launch dependency, and comparison against the control.
3. Document approval checkpoints and measurement with a practical test window.

## Decision checks

Separate the creative proposition from the value of the creator's identity. Confirm which asset, handle, markets, duration and paid usage rights are cleared. Log the expiry and any creator or licensing cost in the test economics.

Compare equivalent placements, audience, dates and offer using the brand and creator identities where feasible. Name the visible identity treatment: brand alone, creator alone, or a partnership with both identities. If the platform chooses which identity to lead with, the observed result belongs to that delivery policy. It cannot identify which handle persuaded the buyer. Keep the asset, copy and permission window matched for a test of identity. Organic reach can justify a hypothesis. Paid incremental purchases or contribution under comparable conditions support an investment decision.

If an authorized course pack is available, consult Partnership Ads and the creator UGC framework for this decision.

## Deliverable

A partnership brief and test plan.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
