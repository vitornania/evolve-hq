# Route an Evolve HQ job

Find the relevant workstream and its next step.

## Inputs

A product and the outcome you want.

## Steps

1. Identify the business decision, current stage, and source of truth.
2. Offer up to three precise skill IDs with required inputs. If the user selects one, preserve their selection and scope.
3. Record the chosen desk, approval needed, and next deliverable.

## Deliverable

A desk assignment and plan for the work.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
