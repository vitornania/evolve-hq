# Write an email sequence

Draft emails for a specific stage of the customer journey.

## Inputs

Audience, offer, trigger, and email goal.

## Steps

1. Decide trigger, segment, goal, and suppression rules before writing copy.
2. Draft the sequence with subject, preheader, message, CTA, interval, and exit condition.
3. Give a quality checklist for links, segments, frequency, consent, and monitoring.

## Decision checks

Give each send one decision and a reason the reader would make it now. For a welcome series, introduce the useful difference, answer the main objection with evidence, then present the verified offer. Tie the CTA to the page that fulfills it.

Map the entry trigger, the delay, and the events that remove someone from the flow. Check for a purchase during the delay before sending an acquisition incentive. Judge revenue per eligible recipient against a comparable holdout when available. Opens alone cannot establish sales impact.

Consult the installed sequence templates and copy guidelines when available.

When choosing the subject and first line, match the promise to what this recipient has already done. A restock request, an unanswered fit question and a routine replenishment can justify different openings even for the same item. Pay off the subject in the email. See [Copy and proof decisions](../evolve-skill/references/book-copy-decisions.md).

## Deliverable

An email sequence with timing and copy.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.

## Decision checks for this skill

Link each offer and proof to the trigger and current landing page. Check a finished preview with actual mobile wrapping before revision.
