# Write an email sequence

Draft emails for a specific stage of the customer journey.

## Inputs

Audience, offer, trigger, and email goal.

## Steps

1. Decide trigger, segment, goal, and suppression rules before writing copy.
2. Draft the sequence with subject, preheader, message, CTA, interval, and exit condition.
3. Give a quality checklist for links, segments, frequency, consent, and monitoring.

## Deliverable

An email sequence with timing and copy.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
