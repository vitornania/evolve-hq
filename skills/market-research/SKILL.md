# Research a product and market

Build an evidence base for product positioning and marketing.

## Inputs

Product brief or URL, market, and available sources.

## Steps

1. Set the decision and target market. Sample customer language and competitor offers separately.
2. Grade each observation by source quality, dates, coverage, and decision relevance. Review the source and the wording in separate passes.
3. Deliver an evidence table, buyer segments, offer gaps, research risks, and recommended next test.

## Deliverable

A reviewed research brief and source list.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
