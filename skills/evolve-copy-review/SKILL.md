# Fix weak ad copy

Diagnose where an existing draft breaks down and rewrite it.

## Inputs

The draft, target audience, and desired action.

## Steps

1. Annotate where the draft loses relevance, clarity, proof, or momentum.
2. Rewrite the smallest passages that fix those issues, preserving substantiated meaning and voice.
3. Give original and revised examples and explain what each revision changes.

## Deliverable

Specific feedback and revised copy.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
