# Fix weak ad copy

Diagnose where an existing draft breaks down and rewrite it.

## Inputs

The draft, target audience, and desired action.

## Steps

1. Annotate where the draft loses relevance, clarity, proof, or momentum.
2. Rewrite the smallest passages that fix those issues, preserving substantiated meaning and voice.
3. Give original and revised examples and explain what each revision changes.

## Decision checks

Read the opening, image and close together and write the claim a reasonable buyer would take away. Check both that claim and the literal wording against the target evidence, including product version, population and timeframe. A qualifier in fine print may leave the main promise intact.

Find the first unsupported leap between desire, mechanism, proof and offer. Show the exact changed passage and its evidence. Preserve locked terms and the campaign CTA, then reread the revised copy on its own. Replace internal evidence commentary with concrete buyer-facing action and remove repeated assertions.

For a disputed lead or central claim, picture the action, identify an observation that could contradict it, and try the same line under the obvious alternative's name. If a grounded line still fits any product, look for a distinctive detail in this product's exact use or construction. A specific detail can earn the line without claiming the brand alone offers it. Keep the diagnosis with the edit notes. Practical offer terms and locked copy can already be doing their job.

For a skeptical audience, distinguish a supported claim placed too early from a claim missing support. Move the relevant demonstration earlier when it resolves the reader’s doubt. Narrow or remove a claim that the demonstration cannot establish. Check a reframed objection against the actual trade-off: state any required tool, buyer effort, eligibility or total price that could change the purchase. A cost-per-use comparison needs a defensible use count and a comparable alternative when savings are claimed. Consult the [direct-response decision guide](../evolve-skill/references/direct-response-decisions.md) when reviewing how an argument earns belief.

If an authorized course pack is available, consult Copywriting Checklist and the Video Script prompt for this decision.

When comparing leads, identify the expectation each creates and the evidence the body must supply. Test a wording change against the same buying reason. See [Copy and proof decisions](../evolve-skill/references/book-copy-decisions.md).

## Deliverable

Specific feedback and revised copy.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.

## Decision checks for this skill

Find the first jump in logic, tailor the smallest fix to the audience’s awareness and compare the changed text to the approved version.
