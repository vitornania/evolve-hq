# Understand your customers

Plan research or synthesize customer interviews, surveys, and feedback.

## Inputs

Research question and any existing responses.

## Steps

1. State the decision and choose an interview, survey, or review synthesis that can answer it.
2. For interviews, ask about actual purchases and alternatives before hypothetical intentions. For synthesis, record themes and exceptions.
3. Return recruitment or sampling notes, questions or findings, and the limits of the evidence.

## Reconstruct the actual decision and its alternatives

1. Ask about the last concrete occasion. Reconstruct what triggered the search, what the buyer used before, why they started looking, what attracted them to a change, and what anxiety or habit nearly stopped them. A question about a real event can distinguish an objection from polite interest.
2. Keep one traceable record per observation with source, date, product or category, speaker when known and exact words or explicitly labeled paraphrase. Flag uncertain identities, reposts, affiliate copy and staff claims. Count records and independent people separately. Do not turn a category observation into a product endorsement.
3. Compare the strongest pattern with an exception or disconfirming case. Explain which choice it might change and what new interview, behavior or transaction could challenge it. Attach evidence IDs so creative can check the original meaning before using any language publicly.

For a purchase-journey interview, give the buyer room to tell the story from the first occasion through the order before probing specific gaps. An unusually large or repeat order can reveal a use case worth investigating. Record how the person was selected, then check the explanation with a contrasting ordinary, first-time or non-buying cohort before describing it as typical.

When several motives fit a quote, reconstruct the last real choice and its alternatives. Identify which buyer answer or observed action would separate the explanations. See [Buyer behavior and evidence](../evolve-skill/references/book-customer-decisions.md).

Apply the relevant checks in [Choose the next creative or research test](../evolve-skill/references/market-and-creative-test-decisions.md#choose-the-next-creative-or-research-test).

## Deliverable

A research plan or themes backed by feedback.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.

## Decision checks for this skill

Invite interviewees to reconstruct the last time the problem happened. Ask about alternatives before showing the product.
