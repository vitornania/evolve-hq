# Map buyer desires and solution beliefs

Find the desires and solution beliefs behind purchase decisions.

## Inputs

Product, market, and customer research sources.

## Steps

1. Trace the buyer's situation, desired end state, current solution, and reason it falls short.
2. Separate a real desire from beliefs about what makes a solution work. Track supporting evidence and contradictions.
3. Propose an evidence-based message test and describe the evidence still needed.

## Deliverable

A structured buyer motivation brief and next test.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
