# Find visual metaphors for an ad

Propose and rank visual ways to show a benefit or objection.

## Inputs

Customer insight, product, and visual constraints.

## Steps

1. Lock one target buyer's situation, desired change and verified product behavior. Complete this sentence privately: 'Seeing this image should make the buyer understand …'. Separate that idea from anything the visual could accidentally promise about speed, safety, certainty or how the product works.
2. Give the requested number of distinct concepts, defaulting to three for exploration. For each, describe its first frame, literal action, intended takeaway, likely alternative reading and a visible product detail that resolves confusion. Specify a practical shot or composition that keeps the actual product and recognizable packaging visibly connected to the metaphor. If the supplied product photo is missing, hand off a concept or an asset request rather than depicting invented packaging.
3. Compare concepts for fit and comprehension before presentation polish. Show an unlabelled thumbnail to a relevant viewer for a brief glance and ask what happened and what the product does. If the interpretation implies an unsupported guarantee, redraw before testing persuasion. For a controlled test, keep the offer, audience, claim and destination stable.

Read the metaphor as a buyer might. If the visual suggests a delivery speed, comparative result or mechanism, supply corresponding product evidence or choose a different visual. See [Copy and proof decisions](../evolve-skill/references/book-copy-decisions.md).

## Deliverable

Return ranked concept cards, or one resolved concept when requested, with the first frame, misread and cheap comprehension check. Use the installed metaphor lab and production-and-testing references for deeper development.

## Decision checks for this skill

Test whether the viewed image alone implies an unsupported capability. Keep the concept only when the metaphor communicates the chosen supported takeaway.
