# Find visual metaphors for an ad

Propose and rank visual ways to show a benefit or objection.

## Inputs

Customer insight, product, and visual constraints.

## Steps

1. Start with one buyer insight and a truthful product benefit.
2. Create three visually distinct metaphors with subject, action, contrast, and an understandable first frame.
3. Rank by comprehension, relevance, and production effort. Flag potential claim confusion.

## Deliverable

Ranked concepts with clear visual directions.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
