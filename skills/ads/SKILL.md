# Plan or troubleshoot paid ads

Review the structure, targeting, and creative of a campaign.

## Inputs

Platform, goal, campaign setup, and any performance data.

## Steps

1. Map setup, objective, audience, creatives, measurement, and economics.
2. When troubleshooting, inspect the largest plausible constraint before changing budget or targeting.
3. Recommend a short test sequence with bounded changes and measurements.

## Deliverable

A campaign plan or diagnostic checklist.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
