# Profile competing offers

Capture competing products, pricing, terms, and positioning.

## Inputs

Product, target market, and competitor names or links.

## Steps

1. Treat direct competitors and substitutes as distinct categories.
2. Capture market, currency, date, pack size, price, guarantee, delivery terms, and source for each comparable offer.
3. Normalize units where appropriate and flag claims or terms that could not be inspected.

## Deliverable

Dated profiles with like-for-like comparisons.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
