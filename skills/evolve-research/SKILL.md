# Run Evolve research

Build an evidence base for a product or a focused question.

## Inputs

Product details and the research question.

## Steps

1. Choose the evidence needed for the user's product decision before gathering sources.
2. Map buyers, problems, alternatives, proof, and market claims. Attach actual source IDs to each major point.
3. Choose a next step within the evidence and list what remains unproven.

## Decision checks

For a full product research job, retain the course sequence of Desire Hunt, New Mechanisms, New Information and Ad Angles. Give each pass its relevant prior findings and source IDs. Before the angle pass, reconcile buyer language and the core audience with subgroups that differ in situation, objection or required proof. For a narrower job, state which decision and pass the user requested. If the user asks for the full course-format report, preserve the installed guide’s worksheet set, rendered-page target and two review passes. Disclose any unmet requirement.

Keep product facts, buyer observations, competitor patterns and hypotheses distinguishable. A plausible mechanism or a repeated competitor claim still needs product-specific support. Pass downstream the strongest evidence, material counterevidence and the unresolved question that affects the next decision.

If an authorized course pack is available, consult Full Product Research and its four original prompts for this decision.

When several motives fit a quote, reconstruct the last real choice and its alternatives. Identify which buyer answer or observed action would separate the explanations. See [Buyer behavior and evidence](../evolve-skill/references/book-customer-decisions.md).

## Deliverable

A research dossier or focused findings.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
