# Run Evolve research

Build an evidence base for a product or a focused question.

## Inputs

Product details and the research question.

## Steps

1. Choose the evidence needed for the user's product decision before gathering sources.
2. Map buyers, problems, alternatives, proof, and market claims. Attach actual source IDs to each major point.
3. Choose a next step within the evidence and list what remains unproven.

## Deliverable

A research dossier or focused findings.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
