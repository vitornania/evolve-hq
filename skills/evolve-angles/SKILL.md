# Find ad angles to test

Turn customer insights into distinct creative hypotheses.

## Inputs

Product facts and research or customer evidence.

## Steps

1. Select buyer tensions with distinct causes or decision barriers.
2. For each angle, write the buyer, promise, proof, opening idea, and predicted response.
3. Rank the options and identify a clean comparison that tests one meaningful difference.

## Decision checks

Start with a buyer situation, the outcome they already want and what they currently believe will deliver it. Develop angles that change the reason for buying. Two phrasings of the same reason belong under the same angle as hook variants.

Before choosing a hook or a swipe, select the product action that can deliver the desired outcome for this buyer on this occasion. A material or specification can explain how the action works. It can also lead when the buyer is explicitly choosing by size, fit or another verified specification. Keep the opening focused on one buying reason. A clever reference headline cannot supply missing demand or product proof.

For each distinct angle, name the proof the buyer would accept and the observation that would make the angle less plausible. Select one to test against the current control and carry the same desire and evidence into the brief.

When a supported belief is blocking the decision, compare a recognizable story, a verified new fact and a different way to interpret a familiar experience as candidate approaches. Keep only approaches that give the buyer distinct reasons to reconsider. If the evidence points to price, delivery or checkout, route the issue to that decision. Keep new messages in one comparable test and reserve opening-only changes for a hook test.

Use the original How to Come Up With Ad Angles prompt where available. Consult the Hook Library when examples bear on the chosen angle. A short, evidence-based angle selection can advance with the sources it has. Exact completion of an original prompt or template depends on its required inputs.

When two explanations predict the same words or clicks, preserve both until a source distinguishes them. Name the finding that would reverse the angle. See [Buyer behavior and evidence](../evolve-skill/references/book-customer-decisions.md).

Apply the relevant checks in [Choose a format from buyer and product evidence](../evolve-skill/references/market-and-creative-test-decisions.md#choose-the-format-from-buyer-and-product-evidence).

## Deliverable

Angles with audiences, reasoning, and test ideas.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
