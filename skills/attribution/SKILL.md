# Understand where sales came from

Compare what different measurement methods imply about revenue.

## Inputs

Channel results, windows, and how attribution is defined.

## Steps

1. Normalize time windows and definitions across platform and first-party sources.
2. Compare what each method measures. Document data gaps, duplicate credit, and modeled results.
3. Report a decision range and propose a measurement check that improves confidence.

## Deliverable

A measurement comparison and gaps to resolve.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
