# Plan static Figma ads

Turn a customer insight into a layout and production specification.

## Inputs

Brief, product assets, dimensions, and brand guidance.

## Steps

1. Draft a clear frame at the requested size with visual hierarchy, safe space, and a primary message.
2. Specify assets, typography, spacing, variants, and accessibility details. Use supplied artwork or approved references.
3. Deliver a precise production brief, or a connected Figma handoff when requested and available.

## Deliverable

A layout and design brief. Finished Figma work needs the Figma connection.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
