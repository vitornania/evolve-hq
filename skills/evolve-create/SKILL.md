# Develop an ad concept

Take a new angle, reference ad, or existing ad into production planning.

## Inputs

A brief or reference, product facts, and constraints.

## Steps

1. Identify the intended angle, constraints, and creative format.
2. Develop hooks, proof devices, and a practical visual direction. For adaptations, reuse structural ideas while making original copy and imagery.
3. Deliver a presentation-ready concept, supporting evidence, and test variable.

## Deliverable

A concept or variation with a creative handoff.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
