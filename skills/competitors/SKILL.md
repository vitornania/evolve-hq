# Improve comparison pages

Evaluate competing alternatives and the pages that present them.

## Inputs

Your product and the competing alternative or page.

## Steps

1. Select the comparison decision a shopper needs to make.
2. Apply consistent criteria such as audience fit, product capabilities, total price, and support. Source material claims and note where alternatives win.
3. Draft a balanced comparison page structure and a verification checklist.

## Write a comparison the buyer can verify

1. Name the decision occasion and two or three criteria that change the choice for that reader. Build a dated fact sheet by plan, edition, region, quantity and purchase frequency. Use identical terms for both options and cite the original offer or product documentation for factual claims.
2. Translate each supported difference into a practical consequence. Include a concrete scenario where the competitor or familiar workaround fits better. Mark unverified features unknown. Avoid declaring a universal winner from one criterion.
3. Use the opening to orient the buyer and the close to direct the right-fit reader to a matching offer, demo or specification. Keep reusable comparison data separate from marketing prose so an offer change can be updated across pages.

## Deliverable

A comparison outline, gap analysis, or draft.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
