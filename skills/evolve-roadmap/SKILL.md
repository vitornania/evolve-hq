# Plan your creative roadmap

Organize controls, variations, and future ad batches.

## Inputs

Existing ads, test notes, and upcoming priorities.

## Steps

1. Assign every control and draft a stable creative ID.
2. Build a queue of batches with parent creative, test variable, milestones, assets, and result entry.
3. Show blockers and the next batch using evidence from completed tests.

## Decision checks

Use a stable parent concept ID and separate IDs for creative versions or tests. Keep each learning question, owner, dependency, audience, decision metric and review date attached when work changes hands.

Record briefed, built, live and evaluated states only when their respective evidence exists. Keep a blocked dependency visible and reschedule downstream work without rewriting prior results. Close a test with the observation and the next decision, even if the result is inconclusive.

If an authorized course pack is available, consult Growth Guide and original ad naming templates for this decision.

Choose a follow-up that can distinguish a change in buyer mix or unit economics from a change in creative effectiveness. Carry the original denominator and cohort window into the test. See [Offers, positioning and acquisition](../evolve-skill/references/book-offer-decisions.md).

Apply the relevant checks in [Choose the next creative or research test](../evolve-skill/references/market-and-creative-test-decisions.md#choose-the-next-creative-or-research-test).

When updating from a result tracker, treat a later cumulative record as a new reporting window for the same creative. Preserve earlier observations and avoid counting a second win unless the data identifies a separate test.

## Deliverable

A list of batches, assets, and next tests.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
