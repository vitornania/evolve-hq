# Plan creator content

Develop a creator sourcing, production, and feedback plan.

## Inputs

Product, target buyer, budget constraints, and content needs.

## Steps

1. Define creator selection criteria, audience, deliverables, and usage rights to clarify.
2. Prepare a brief with opening options, proof requirements, filming guidance, and a review timeline.
3. Provide a tracking table for consent, assets, feedback, and approvals.

## Decision checks

Separate exact product facts and offer language from lines the creator can express in their own words. Only a genuine user can recount a personal result. Provide a demonstration route when first-person experience or outcome substantiation is missing.

Request the specific framing, shot length, visible proof, clean audio and caption-safe areas needed for editing. Confirm usage rights, music clearance and expiry. Accept the take only when the demonstrated action and spoken takeaway agree with the approved brief.

If an authorized course pack is available, consult Creator selection tracker and the creator UGC framework for this decision.

## Use the trial to decide on repeat work

For a sourcing decision, distinguish the ability to supply usable footage from the ability to deliver profitable paid ads. A sample or organic post can earn a trial. Evaluate a trial against the requested buyer situation, usable demonstration, delivery reliability and cleared placements. Before recommending repeat or retained work, look for repeatable paid contribution after creator costs across comparable assets, or name what the next brief must test. Tie the next brief to a missing scene, angle or demonstration visible in the editor’s shot list. Keep examples of retainers or commissions separate from the brand’s actual agreement.

For a creator demonstration, establish what this speaker can know firsthand and what the shot can actually show. Keep any measured result tied to the conditions shown, including when the creator reads from an approved brief. See [Copy and proof decisions](../evolve-skill/references/book-copy-decisions.md).

## Deliverable

Creator criteria and a content collection plan.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.

## Decision checks for this skill

Brief creator perspective, one action and one proof moment per takeaway. Include pickup clips and exact offer overlay separately.
