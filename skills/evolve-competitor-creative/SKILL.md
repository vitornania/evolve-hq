# Study competitor ads

Identify what competing ads emphasize across hooks, offers, and formats.

## Inputs

Competitor names, ad links, or supplied examples.

## Steps

1. Record advert source, date, market, offer, hook shape, visual device, and CTA.
2. Separate observations from inferred media or performance information.
3. Identify transferable patterns and propose an original variation for the user's product.

## Decision checks

Record the observed ad, placement or format, date, and destination where accessible. Reconstruct the belief that the opening seems to assume and mark that interpretation as a hypothesis. Longevity or repeated appearances establish visibility only.

Identify a transferable element such as question order, visual comparison or proof placement. State which target-product evidence would earn the same role. If the target lacks the competitor's result, endorsement or offer, propose a supportable alternative or omit that execution.

If an authorized course pack is available, consult Company Research Document and Winning Ads examples for this decision.

## Deliverable

An annotated competitor creative review.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
