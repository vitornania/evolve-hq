# Critique an ad or script

Find specific issues in the hook, message, visuals, or call to action.

## Inputs

Ad image, script, or exported frames and audience.

## Steps

1. Inspect supplied frames or script and name the evidence available.
2. Review hook, progression, promise, proof, visual clarity, and action. Complete a second pass for substantiation and fit.
3. Prioritize the next three edits and propose specific replacement lines or shots.

## Deliverable

A prioritized critique and revised creative suggestions.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
