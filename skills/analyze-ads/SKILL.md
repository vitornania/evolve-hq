# Critique an ad or script

Find specific issues in the hook, message, visuals, or call to action.

## Inputs

Ad image, script, or exported frames and audience.

## Steps

1. Inspect supplied frames or script and name the evidence available.
2. Review hook, progression, promise, proof, visual clarity, and action. Complete a second pass for substantiation and fit.
3. Prioritize the next three edits and propose specific replacement lines or shots.

## Separate the viewing experience from the sales result

1. Describe the visible or audible sequence first. For video cite the exact timestamp of each consequential shot or line. For statics note the first readable element at placement size. Trace the opening promise, how the next beat pays it off, the proof and the action. Identify the first moment the intended buyer has to make an unsupported leap.
2. Check which performance evidence actually belongs to this creative. State period, audience, spend, objective, attribution and destination when available. An active ad or a high share count can support an observation about distribution, while profitability needs matched spend and orders.
3. Recommend the smallest revision to that moment, a control to hold fixed, and the measure that would distinguish weak attention from weak belief or a poor landing experience. Preserve what the available evidence supports.

## Deliverable

A prioritized critique and revised creative suggestions.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
