# Organize customer insights

Turn raw evidence into buyer profiles and buying motivations.

## Inputs

Research notes or a source-indexed evidence file.

## Steps

1. Group evidence by the buyer's trigger, friction, belief, and desired outcome.
2. Write compact profiles with evidence IDs and counterexamples. Avoid demographic assumptions the sources cannot establish.
3. Map promising beliefs to message or offer tests.

## Deliverable

Buyer profiles, beliefs, and ad opportunities.

Keep source details with the deliverable. Label assumptions and missing evidence. Preserve the scope and any approvals supplied by the user.
