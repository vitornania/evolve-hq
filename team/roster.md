# Evolve HQ roster

Start with the Chief of Staff, four research sprint roles, Video Editor, and Office Developer. These are task assignments. When the host supports delegation, the existing Market Research Team skill can dispatch independent research tracks. Otherwise the active assistant carries out the roles in order and labels the review as a self-review.

| Customer Researcher | Research & Evidence | Starter team | Record short customer quotes and observations with stable source IDs, context, and dates. Flag gaps and contradictory evidence. |
| Competitor Researcher | Research & Evidence | Starter team | Compare direct offers and substitutes with market, currency, pack size, terms, and dated sources. |

## Research sprint handoff

1. The Chief records the product, market, requested decision and available sources. Use the existing Market Research Team process and applicable Evolve source materials.
2. Customer Researcher and Competitor Researcher return findings with dated source IDs. The Chief keeps products and markets scoped to the job.
3. Evidence Reviewer checks the underlying sources and flags unsupported claims, duplicate evidence and mismatched offers. The Ad Strategist uses reviewed evidence for up to three supported angles.
4. The Chief returns the brief, evidence, gaps, and a recommended next decision. A shared file is a reference until the owner explicitly asks to start a job.
5. Once an angle and a script are chosen, Video Editing receives the product, selected angle and evidence, approved script, and supplied assets. Stage the handoff for review before editing in Resolve.

Keep local drafts and previews reviewable. The owner authorizes external contact, publishing, spending, and live account actions. The Office Developer owns app changes.
